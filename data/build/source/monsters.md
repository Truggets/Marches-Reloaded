# Curated Monster Stat Blocks (M11 sandbox v0)

other bundled SRD category builds from: `downfallx/dnd-5e-srd-markdown` at
commit `1b4b99dcb786cdd1a2fb26f8acec1551191f1ca4` (CC-BY 4.0), sourced from its
`monsters-A-Z.md` and `animals.md` files. This is a hand-picked subset (not the
full ~650KB combined bestiary) — see docs/planning/m11-sandbox-v0-plan.md.
